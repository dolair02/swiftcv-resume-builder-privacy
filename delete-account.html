Account deletion

Delete your SwiftCV account

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This page explains how to ask us to delete your SwiftCV account and the data stored for it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email dolair02@gmail.com from the email address of your SwiftCV account.
  2. Use the subject “SwiftCV account deletion request”.
  3. We confirm your request by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email address we hold for it.
  • Everything saved with your account: your résumés, cover letters and job tracker entries.

What is kept

  • Once the deletion is complete, we no longer hold your account data.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google’s policies. Deleting your SwiftCV account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > SwiftCV).

More information

See the Privacy Policy for what SwiftCV collects and how it is used.
