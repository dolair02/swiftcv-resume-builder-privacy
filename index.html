Privacy Policy for SwiftCV

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the SwiftCV mobile application collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. By using SwiftCV, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Account information. Creating an account requires an email address. We store your email address and an internal user ID so that we can authenticate you and associate your documents with your account.

Resume content. The information you enter into a resume — including your name, contact details, work history, education, and skills — is stored on our servers so that your documents are saved between sessions and available across devices. This content is private to your account and is not published, shared, or made searchable.

Technical and diagnostic data. We may collect limited technical information such as crash reports and error logs to diagnose problems and improve stability.

Purchases. SwiftCV Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles your payment; we never see or store your card or payment details.

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in
  • To generate, save, and export the documents you create
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve reliability
  • To respond to support requests you send us

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. We do not share your resume content with recruiters, employers, or any other third party.

How Information Is Shared

We share personal information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. We use third-party hosting and infrastructure providers to operate the app. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law or valid legal process.

Data Security

Information transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information and saved documents for as long as your account remains active. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time on our delete-account page or by emailing dolair02@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We process deletion requests within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play.

Children's Privacy

SwiftCV is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to dolair02@gmail.com.
